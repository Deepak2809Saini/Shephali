About Me

Where craft meets construction.

I'm Shephali Agrawal, a fashion design student currently pursuing my B.Sc in Fashion Designing at IIS University, Jaipur. My work sits at the intersection of illustration, pattern, and textile — shaped by growing up in a city known for its block prints, embroidery, and handloom traditions.

I completed my Higher Secondary schooling at Subodh Public School, Jaipur in 2024, before moving into a course that lets me build on that foundation with hands-on garment construction, draping, and surface ornamentation. I care about the full journey of a garment — from the first sketch to the finished silhouette.
